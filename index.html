About

I'm Ivan. I like turning ideas into prototypes I can hold, test, and sometimes break.

Since 2025 that has meant a reaction-wheel testbed at the Philippine Space Agency, origami antennas and live satellite passes in Tokyo, and now attitude control work in Taipei. What ties it together is how small satellites move: how one knows where it's pointing, and how several can fly together.

Long term, I want to help design interplanetary spacecraft.

Papers and talks

Four conference papers in 2026, one as first author. Flip a card to read its abstract.

  1. Nov 2026First author

    Development of an Origami Antenna Deployment Mechanism Accommodating Substrate Thickness for Nanosatellites

    15th Nano-Satellite Symposium+ (NSAT+), Tainan, Taiwan

    Read abstract

    AbstractFirst author

    This paper presents a deployment mechanism for origami-folded antennas on nanosatellites that accounts for the real thickness of the antenna substrate. Ideal zero-thickness folding patterns do not fold cleanly once the material has thickness, so the mechanism adjusts the fold geometry to keep stowage compact and deployment reliable. The paper describes the design approach and how it was evaluated.

  2. Nov 2026Co-author

    Evaluation of the Stowage and Deployment Characteristics of a Membrane-Based Reconfigurable Reflectarray Antenna Structure

    70th Space Sciences and Technology Conference, Utsunomiya, Japan

    Read abstract

    AbstractCo-author

    This study examines how a membrane-based reconfigurable reflectarray antenna stows and deploys. It looks at the folded configuration, the deployment sequence, and how well the deployed surface keeps the shape the antenna needs, with the aim of informing future membrane antennas for small satellites.

  3. Nov 2026Co-author

    On-Orbit Attitude Data Analysis of the OrigamiSat-2 CubeSat with a Deployable Membrane Antenna Structure

    70th Space Sciences and Technology Conference, Utsunomiya, Japan (accepted)

    Read abstract

    AbstractCo-author

    This paper analyzes attitude telemetry from OrigamiSat-2, a CubeSat carrying a deployable membrane antenna structure. It reviews the satellite's attitude behavior on orbit and discusses how the deployed membrane affects the spacecraft's dynamics, giving flight data to compare against pre-launch expectations.

    77th International Astronautical Congress (IAC 2026), Antalya, Türkiye
